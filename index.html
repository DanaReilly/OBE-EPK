A film by Dana Reilly

Three elite women bodybuilders—Ariel, Amanda, and Chelsea—compete to qualify for the Ms. Olympia, the most coveted muscle show title. Together, their journeys reveal what it costs to strive for strength in a world that tells women to stay small.
